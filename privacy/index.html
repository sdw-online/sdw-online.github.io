Privacy Policy: SDW Media Automation

Last updated: 14 October 2026

SDW Media Automation is a private, single-user automation tool run by Stephen David-Williams for his own accounts. On the Google sign-in screen it appears under the OAuth client name "Claude Code", after the AI coding assistant used to operate it. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than its owner signs in to it.

Google data the app accesses

  • Gmail: read-only access to messages and attachments (for example payment remittances), and Gmail filter settings.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: reading and writing the owner's own files.
  • Google Calendar: reading and managing the owner's own events.
  • YouTube: managing the owner's own channel (video descriptions, comments) and reading channel statistics and YouTube Analytics.
  • Basic profile: email address and name, to confirm which account is signed in.

How the data is used

Data is used only to run the owner's own content and admin workflows. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models, and it is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Storage

OAuth tokens and any downloaded data are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database of other people's information.

Limited Use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled in line with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app immediately; locally stored tokens then no longer work.
